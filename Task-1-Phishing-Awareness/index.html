What is phishing

Phishing is a social engineering cyberattack where scammers use fraudulent messages to trick people into revealing sensitive data—like passwords and credit card numbers—or installing malware

Phishing Emails

Bulk fraudulent emails sent to thousands of people pretending to be banks, stores, or service providers.

Fake websites

A fake website is a fraudulent lookalike webpage created by cybercriminals to mimic legitimate brands, banks, or services. Its primary goal is to deceive users into giving away sensitive information like passwords, credit cards, or personal data.

Social Engineering

Social engineering is a psychological manipulation technique cybercriminals use to trick people into giving away confidential information or access.

How to spot a phishing attack

High Urgency

Forcing you to act immediately (e.g., "Your account will be permanently deleted in 15 minutes!").

High Urgency

Forcing you to act immediately (e.g., "Your account will be permanently deleted in 15 minutes!").

Greed or Excitement

Offering unexpected financial rewards, free luxury goods, or job offers that require no effort.

Over-Sharing or Direct Inquiries

Asking for sensitive information (like OTPs, passwords, or employee IDs) that a legitimate company or manager would already have or never request over message.

Inconsistent Stories

Someone claiming to be a close friend or executive but using odd phrasing, poor grammar, or an uncharacteristically formal/informal tone.

Safety Tips

Verify the sender independently.
Never share secrets like OTPs.
Use a password manager with strong multi-factor authentication.
Monitor account activity.
Turn off auto-download.
Deny unexpected MFA prompts.
